## What actually broke

The relay on your Mac has not run successfully since August 20. Both shows are affected — Monday's Market Mondays routine hit the same wall yesterday and had to build its report from secondhand recaps.

The most likely cause is timing. The `com.kennethalford.show-transcript-tt` job is set to fire Wednesday at 00:00 Central. The show streams Tuesday evening and runs three hours or more, so it often ends near midnight — and YouTube needs a further hour or two to generate auto-captions on a three-hour video. At 00:00 the captions frequently do not exist yet.

There are two ways that plays out, and they leave different fingerprints:

If the job fired and found no captions, the script's `fail()` path would have pushed you an alert reading "no subtitles available." If you got that alert overnight, that is the answer.

If you got no alert at all, the job probably never fired — a sleeping Mac defers a `StartCalendarInterval` job until it wakes. In that case the fetch will run whenever you open your laptop, and Episode 207's transcript may well land in the repository later today.

That second case is worth acting on: if the transcript shows up today, ask me for the recap and I will write the full report against it. Nothing is lost, it is just late.

## Worth fixing when you're at the Mac

Give the captions more room, and take more than one swing at it. `StartCalendarInterval` accepts an array of times, so the job can retry at 00:30, 01:00, 01:30 and 02:00 Central instead of firing once at midnight. The script already exits cleanly when the transcript is current, so extra runs cost nothing. Keep the last attempt before about 02:00, since this routine reads the repository around 02:30.

Also make sure the Mac is awake for it — `sudo pmset repeat wake MTWRFSU 23:45:00` will wake it before the window opens.

One quieter problem worth knowing about: when the newest item on the channel still looks like the previous episode, the script logs "already current" and exits successfully without alerting you. A missed week produces silence rather than an error. That is exactly what silence looked like this morning, and it is why you did not hear about it until now.

The log is at `~/.local/state/cloud-routines-relay-logs/fetch.log`, and the launchd output at `launchd-tt.log` in the same folder. Those two files will tell you which of the cases above happened.
